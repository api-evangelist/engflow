Benchmarking any2bazel - towards more efficient migrations We’re fond of Bazel, and particularly fond of the capabilities it offers developers. Faster builds, made more efficient through remote execution and caching, made more understandable through declarative configuration, queryability, and precise logging – these deliver benefits and solutions to developers once Bazel’s all set up and integrated into their daily processes. But it’s a rare developer who enjoys spending their time and effort in build system configuration.
